Tools the bot uses
    
        
  • ✓Live data feed: PumpPortal WebSocket with every new launch and each trade on the tokens being watched.
    • 
        
  • ✓Bonding-curve model: constant-product math (x·y=k) for fills, so buys and sells include price impact.
    • 
        
  • ✓Wallet tracker: follows the dev wallet, counts unique buyers, spots early bundles and big single wallets.
    • 
        
  • ✓Filter and score engine: the entry rules on the left, applied to every launch.
    • 
        
  • ✓Risk manager: trade size, max open, max exposure, daily loss stop, loss-streak cooldown, rate limit.
    • 
        
  • ✓Exit manager: stop loss, take-profit ladder, breakeven stop, trailing stop, time and dev-sell exits.
    • 
        
  • ✓Paper executor: fees, priority fee, latency and slippage cap on every fill.
    • 
        
  • ✓Journal, recorder and backtester: every trade is logged; record the live feed and replay it with new settings.
    • 
        
  • ✗Not included (needed for real money): a Solana wallet and private key, a paid RPC node, a trade API or direct program calls, priority fees or Jito tips, a server running 24/7 instead of a browser tab, and a remote kill switch. Real trading is not built in.

Quick backtest on simulated launches

      
Generates launches with a mix of rugs (some dumped through hidden dev wallets), duds, bundles, fake-volume pumps and rare runners, then runs your current strategy over them in a few seconds. The mix is invented. It tests the logic, not the real market. A good result here is not evidence the bot makes money.
