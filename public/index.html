How it works

      
    
        
  1. You sign in with your own wallet. It signs a message, never a transaction you did not see.
    2. 
        
  2. The bot reads prices, applies the moving-average crossover, and produces a proposal with a written reason.
    3. 
        
  3. Paper mode records the fill in this app's own database. Devnet mode queues a draft for your wallet to sign; this build never builds or sends a mainnet transaction.
    4. 
        
  4. Every proposal passes your per-trade cap and daily loss cap first, and STOP halts everything.
    5. 
        
  5. The paid API charges a fixed number of credits per call, from a prepaid ledger. No credits, no call.
    6. 
      

    

    

      
What can go wrong

      
    
        
  • You can lose all the money you put in. This is an experiment, not financial advice.
    • 
        
  • Paper results and past results do not predict real results. Prices here are simulated, not a live exchange feed, unless the operator sets a real RPC.
    • 
        
  • Bugs, fees, slippage, outages and scams (rug pulls, honeypot tokens) lose money.
    • 
        
  • A server key can be stolen; that is why the app never holds one for your funds.
    • 
        
  • Trading may be taxed or restricted where you live.
    • 
        
  • Mainnet with real funds is switched off. Real payments are switched off; top-ups here are fake money.
    • 
      

      
Full detail: RISKS.md and README.md in the repository.
